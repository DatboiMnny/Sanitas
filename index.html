💡 How scores work

Food & supplements are scored out of 100 on what's actually in them, not just nutrition:

  • 🧪 Additives — 25
  • 🥗 Nutrition (or protein / supplement quality) — 20
  • 🌾 Ingredient quality — 15 (added sugars, seed oils, trans fats, refined flour, fillers)
  • 🏭 Processing — 15
  • 🌱 Pesticides — 8 · ⚗️ Heavy metals — 8 · 🧴 Microplastics — 4
  • 🍺 Alcohol — −15 to −35, capped at 49 (shows % alcohol and standard drinks)
  • 🙋 Fit for you — 5 (your goals, diet & allergies)
  • 🐾 Animal welfare — up to −10 (caged eggs, factory-farmed meat, unsustainable fish, palm oil; cruelty-free status for personal care). Can be turned off.

Loose fruit & veg: type the number on the sticker (e.g. 4011 for bananas; organic starts with 9).

👕 Clothing is scored from the care label: organic & certified (10), chemicals & finishes (25), microplastics (20), environment (20), skin & comfort (25), plus animal welfare.

Pet food is scored on pet safety (toxic ingredients), ingredient quality, preservatives & colours, and protein. Household products on health & safety, irritants, environment and packaging.

Personal care is scored on hormone disruptors, irritants, other health concerns, microplastics and skin fit.

80+ Excellent · 60+ Good · 40+ Mediocre · 20+ Poor · below 20 Bad
