Common questions

  • A product isn't found. Tap Add it from the label — snap the ingredients and nutrition panel and Sanitas remembers it. You can also add it to Open Food Facts so it works for everyone.
  • The camera won't scan. Allow camera access in Settings › Sanitas. Hold the phone 15–20 cm away in good light, or use Photo of barcode.
  • Clothing. Clothes don't have a public database, so Sanitas reads the care label (fibres and percentages) instead of the barcode.
  • Where do the scores come from? See Sources & methodology.
  • Privacy. No account, no tracking — see the privacy policy.

Sanitas gives general information only and is not medical advice.
